What's Included

Certified frames and lenses

A curated range of ANSI Z87.1 frames in prescription, with side shields, anti-fog, polarized and photochromic options. Every pair is marked and documented for your safety files.

Company pricing and allowances

We set a per-employee allowance with you, and employees see exactly what the company covers at checkout. Anything above it, they can pay themselves.

Online ordering portal

Your team orders from their phone or a shop computer. Online fitting software captures measurements, so nobody is filling in a paper form and faxing it back.

Prescriptions and exams

Employees can upload a current prescription or we can coordinate an eye exam. Either way, the lens work happens in our lab.

Nationwide shipping

Ship to one address or straight to individual sites. Most prescription orders leave the lab within a few days.

Repairs and replacements

Frames get dropped and lenses get scratched on a job site. Replacements come from the same lab, on the same short turnaround.

Who It's For

Safety and HR teams who are tired of chasing a national supplier

Mining, construction, manufacturing, aviation, drilling, fabrication — anywhere a crew needs prescription eyewear that holds up and paperwork that doesn't pile up. Whether you have five employees in glasses or five hundred, we build the program to match how you already work.
