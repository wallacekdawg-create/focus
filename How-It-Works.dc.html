How It Works

Three steps from a sample pair to a program your team actually uses

Most of the work is on our side. Here's what happens after you ask for a sample, and what we'll need from you along the way.

Get My Free Sample Pair

Step 01

Claim your free sample pair

Fill in the short form and we'll send a non-prescription sample pair, no cost and no commitment. Look at the build, check the Z87.1 markings, hand it around the shop and see what your crew thinks of it.

What we need: your name, company, and how many people wear glasses

Step 02

We build your company's eyewear program and pricing

One call is usually enough. We go through your hazards, your current supplier's pricing, the frame styles you want available and the allowance you want to set. Then we put together a written program with your pricing, your approved frames and the documentation your safety files need.

Typical timeline: a few days from first call to a program you can sign off on

Step 03

Your team orders online — we handle the rest

Employees pick their frames in the portal, upload a prescription or book an exam, and complete their fitting online. We cut the lenses in our lab and ship the finished pairs to your site. Replacements and repairs run through the same portal, so HR isn't in the middle of it.

Turnaround: most prescription orders ship within a few days

What Changes For You

Days, not weeks

Lens work happens in our own lab, so nothing sits in another company's queue.

One point of contact

The same person who sets up your program answers the phone when something goes wrong.

Paperwork off your desk

Orders, fittings and compliance records live in the portal, ready when you need them.
